International lives

Every firm has a beginning. Ours started in the Dutch maritime community. For more than seventy years, Tysma | Lems has worked alongside shipowners, offshore businesses and maritime professionals whose careers and businesses have always extended beyond national borders. As trade routes expanded and the industry became increasingly international, so did the tax and social security challenges that accompanied it.

Rather than changing direction, we grew with our clients.

Today, that same international perspective continues to guide our international tax support and advisory services. The maritime world remains part of our identity, providing the foundation from which we now advise globally mobile professionals, international businesses and private clients around the world.

Because international lives have always required international thinking.
